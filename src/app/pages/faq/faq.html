Getting started

What do I need to open a PayDay wallet?

A phone number registered in Cameroon and a valid ID: national identity card, passport or residence permit. Verification usually completes in under five minutes.

Can I use PayDay without a bank account?

Yes. You can fund your wallet entirely from MTN MoMo or Orange Money, and withdraw at any agent location. A bank account is optional and only needed for large settlements.

Which devices does the app support?

Android 8 and above, iOS 14 and above. If your phone is older, the web wallet works in any modern browser and supports the same transfers.

Payments & transfers

How long does a transfer take?

PayDay-to-PayDay transfers settle in about six seconds. Transfers to MTN MoMo or Orange Money depend on the network and normally complete within two minutes, or fifteen during peak hours.

My transfer says "pending". What now?

Pending means the receiving network has not confirmed yet. Wait fifteen minutes before acting: the money stays in your account and is never debited twice. If it is still pending after that, send us the reference shown on the transaction and we will trace it.

Which bills can I pay?

ENEO electricity, Camwater, Canal+ subscriptions, airtime and data bundles for all networks. Bill payments post to the provider the same day.

Can I accept payments for my business?

Yes. A merchant account gives you a payment link, a QR code for the counter, and a REST API with test keys. Settlement runs daily to your wallet or bank account.

Fees & limits

What does PayDay cost?

Opening and holding a wallet is free. Sending to another PayDay wallet is free. Cash-out and cross-network transfers carry a single fee shown on the confirmation screen before you approve.

Are there transaction limits?

A verified personal wallet can send up to FCFA 1,000,000 per day and hold up to FCFA 5,000,000. Merchant accounts are set higher after review.

Do you charge for an inactive account?

No. There are no dormancy fees, no monthly charges and no minimum balance.

Security

How are my funds protected?

Customer balances are held with partner banks and kept separate from company money. PayDay operates under CEMAC regulation and is audited annually.

I lost my phone. What should I do?

Dial #237# from any line linked to your account to freeze the wallet, then call support to confirm your identity. Your balance stays intact while the wallet is frozen.

Will PayDay ever ask for my PIN?

Never. No employee, agent or support message will ask for your PIN or a one-time code. Anyone who does is attempting fraud, and you should report it to us immediately.

Still stuck?

Send the details to our support team and we will reply within a day.
