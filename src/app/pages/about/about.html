OUR STORY

Money that moves at the speed of Sound

PayDay started in Buea with a simple frustration: sending money between a bank account, MTN MoMo and Orange Money took three apps, two fees and far too much patience. We built one wallet that speaks to all of them.

Built here, for here

We are a team of engineers, agents and support staff spread across Buea, Douala and Yaoundé. Every feature we ship is tested on the networks our customers actually use, on the devices they actually carry.

That means designing for patchy connections, for airtime-first habits and for the shop owner who needs settlement before the market closes. It also means no hidden charges: you see the fee before you confirm, every time.

  • Licensed and supervised under CEMAC regulations
  • Funds held with partner banks, never lent out
  • Support in English and French, seven days a week

What we hold ourselves to

Four commitments that decide what we build, and what we refuse to build.

Safety first

Encryption in transit and at rest, device binding, and a fraud team that reviews unusual activity before it reaches you.

Speed you can plan around

Transfers settle in seconds, not hours, and we publish our timings instead of hiding behind "up to 24 hours".

Clear pricing

One fee, shown before you confirm. No monthly charges, no dormancy penalties, no surprises on the statement.

People, not tickets

A real person answers. Agents are trained on the same systems our engineers use, so answers are accurate the first time.

How we got here

A short history, in the order it happened.

  1. 2022

    A prototype in Molyko

    Four developers build a bridge between two mobile money APIs to settle a student association's dues.

  2. 2023

    First licence

    PayDay is approved as a payment service provider and opens its first agent network in Buea and Limbe.

  3. 2024

    Bills and merchants

    ENEO and Camwater payments go live, followed by merchant tools and a public developer API.

  4. Today

    Ten thousand wallets

    More than 10,000 people move money with PayDay each month across 1,200 agent locations.
